## Awareness in Decoded Neurofeedback: Semantic Analyses of Verbal Reports and Meta-Analytic Evidence from Forced-Choice Judgments

This repository contains the code and data used to investigate participants' awareness in Decoded Neurofeedback(DecNef) studies. We performed:
<ul>
    <li> A meta-analysis of post-training forced-choice identification tasks to evaluate explicit awareness of the trained target.</li>
    <li> Large language model (LLM)-based semantic analyses of participants' verbal strategy reports to assess whether they contained information related to the DecNef target or trained condition.</li>
</ul>

Our analyses suggest that while participants generally could not explicitly identify the trained target, their verbal reports contained semantic information associated with the experimental condition, supporting the idea that awareness may be indirect rather than fully explicit.
